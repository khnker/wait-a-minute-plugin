# Surface Archetypes

Choose the dominant archetype from the user's primary job. Mixed pages may contain secondary archetypes, but the first viewport needs one leader.

## Task-Led

Examples: login, signup, checkout, booking, search, upload, application, submission, recovery.

Protagonist: the task, its context, and current state.

Required regions:

- inputs, selectors, or provider actions;
- selected account, organization, product, appointment, or target when relevant;
- primary action and recovery path;
- error, denied, locked, loading, success, and retry states as appropriate;
- support, policy, device, session, deadline, or consequence context when useful.

## Command-Led

Examples: dashboards, admin tools, monitors, editors, operational consoles.

Protagonist: current state plus available action.

Required regions: orientation, toolbar, filters, status, data/table/chart, selection, detail, feedback.

## Inspection-Led

Examples: product details, report preview, documentation, article, portfolio item, profile, invoice.

Protagonist: the entity, its data, and its story.

## Conversion-Led

Examples: landing pages, product overview, services page, marketing home.

Protagonist: the value proposition and the call to action.
